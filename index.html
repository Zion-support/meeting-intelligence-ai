Before connecting real customer data

  1. Measure time to prepare notes and actions confirmed by their owners.
  2. Use synthetic examples. Confirm participant consent, retention and access before recording or sharing real meetings.
  3. Participants approve notes. Verify decisions, name action owners and define a stop rule before expanding the pilot.
